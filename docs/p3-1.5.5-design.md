## Art direction / implementation
One dominant place: an EMPTY blue-hour coastal carriage/window memory. Three perspective window openings and a receding sill/seat plane establish location. An original passing shoreline moves seamlessly behind the stationary window frame (48s transform loop). No characters, silhouettes, logos or game-image wallpaper.
Two supporting accents: curved water-light reflections through the upper glass; a very restrained cropped disc reflection at the far lower-right. Existing capsule, moon/clock, arcana, bubbles and water entry remain subordinate and intact. Midnight shifts the outside view toward muted mint while the reading field remains royal/navy blue.
The original SVGs are small, self-contained, locally embedded; no canvas, timers, network requests, video wallpaper or blur filters. Movement uses transforms only; reduced motion gives the full still final scene, not an empty background.

## Three composition rounds
1. Established windows/coast at 1440 and 1024 compact. Critique: too sharp a skyline behind paragraphs; some contour strokes accidentally formed roof-like diagonals.
2. Strengthened the broad central navy reading veil, softened skyline planes, reduced frame stroke and introduced curved water light. Critique: compact stayed readable, but diagonal roof artifacts distracted from the place.
3. Corrected explicit window contour coordinates; kept only meaningful mullions/sill and the single light shaft. Landing has visible place/depth, chat remains quiet, compact preserves the clean white title margin and practical controls. Additional finals cover short windows, popup/settings, native file/tool/status cards and Dark Hour.
